# SureLive

**SureLive Studio · Edge 预览版 / Edge Preview**

## 中文介绍

SureLive 是面向英语视频学习的轻量浏览器扩展。你可以在支持的视频页面查看双语字幕，点击字幕定位到对应内容，并重复播放当前句；也可以打开本地影院，选择自己的视频和 SRT、VTT 或 ASS 字幕，调整播放速度、查看词义并收藏词句。收藏记录与设置保存在浏览器中，Anki 导出可生成 APKG 卡包，媒体能否附带取决于来源可用性和录制条件。

这是首个 Edge 预览版，已验证 YouTube、哔哩哔哩、百度网盘视频页面及本地影院。基于现有的网站适配与通用视频检测能力，理论上支持更多视频网站，但未经验证；具体可用性取决于网页播放器和字幕来源。

**理论支持，未经本版验证：** Netflix、Amazon Prime Video、Disney+、Hulu、Crunchyroll、TVer、Bandai Channel、U-NEXT、Viki、Max / HBO Max、Twitch、Stremio、Plex、Emby、Jellyfin，以及其他带兼容网页播放器的网站。以上网站可通过继承的网站适配或通用视频检测尝试使用，具体功能取决于播放器、字幕来源及网站限制。

提供简体中文、英语、日语、德语、法语、西班牙语和俄语七种界面语言，可选择熟悉的语言辅助英语视频学习。

本地播放兼容性取决于 Edge 支持的音视频编码。在线翻译、词典和自行配置的 AI 服务需要联网，会向相应服务发送相关文本。

## English Overview

SureLive is a lightweight browser extension for learning English with videos. On supported video pages, view bilingual subtitles, click a subtitle to jump to that moment, and repeat the current sentence. In Local Cinema, open your own video and SRT, VTT or ASS subtitles, adjust playback speed, look up words, and save words and sentences. Saved items and settings stay in your browser. Anki export creates APKG decks; included media depends on source availability and recording conditions.

This is the first Edge preview. Verified use covers YouTube, Bilibili, Baidu Netdisk video pages and Local Cinema. Existing website integrations and generic video detection theoretically support additional video websites, but these have not been verified. Actual availability depends on the website's player and subtitle sources.

**Theoretical support — not verified in this release:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Crunchyroll, TVer, Bandai Channel, U-NEXT, Viki, Max / HBO Max, Twitch, Stremio, Plex, Emby, Jellyfin, and other websites with compatible web players. These may work through inherited website integrations or generic video detection; individual features depend on the player, subtitle sources and website restrictions.

Seven interface languages are available: Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, German, French, Spanish and Russian. Choose a familiar interface language to support your English video learning.

Local playback depends on the audio and video codecs supported by Edge. Online translation, dictionaries and configured AI services require internet access and send relevant text to the selected service.

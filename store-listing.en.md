# SureLive Store Listing

## Name And Summary

Name: SureLive - English Learning Assistant

Team: SureLive Studio

Short description: Learn English and multiple languages with video, bilingual subtitles, sentence replay, dictionary lookup, saved items and Anki export.

## Detailed Description

SureLive is a lightweight browser extension for learning English and multiple target languages (Japanese, French, Spanish, German, Chinese) with videos. On supported video pages, view bilingual subtitles, click a subtitle to jump to that moment, and repeat the current sentence. In Local Cinema, open your own video and SRT, VTT or ASS subtitles (or generate subtitles locally with the optional local speech-to-subtitle helper), adjust playback speed, look up words, and save words and sentences. Saved items and settings stay in your browser. Anki export creates APKG decks; included media depends on source availability and recording conditions.

Verified use covers YouTube, Bilibili, Baidu Netdisk video pages and Local Cinema. Existing website integrations and generic video detection theoretically support additional video websites, but these have not been verified. Actual availability depends on the website's player and subtitle sources.

**Theoretical support — not verified in this release:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Crunchyroll, TVer, Bandai Channel, U-NEXT, Viki, Max / HBO Max, Twitch, Stremio, Plex, Emby, Jellyfin, and other websites with compatible web players. These may work through inherited website integrations or generic video detection; individual features depend on the player, subtitle sources and website restrictions.

Seven interface languages are available: Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, German, French, Spanish and Russian. Choose a familiar interface language to support your video language learning.

Local playback depends on the browser's supported audio and video codecs. Online translation, dictionaries, subtitle search and configured AI services require internet access. Subtitle availability and exported media depend on the video source and browser permissions.

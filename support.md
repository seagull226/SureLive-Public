# SureLive Support

Use this page for SureLive support and bug-report guidance.

## Before reporting an issue

Please check that:

- you are using a supported Chromium-based browser;
- SureLive is updated to the latest released version;
- the video page has loaded normally;
- required subtitles or media are available for the feature you are using.

## What to include in a bug report

Please include:

- SureLive version;
- browser name and version;
- website where the issue occurred;
- exact steps that reproduce the problem;
- what you expected;
- what actually happened;
- screenshots or a short screen recording where useful.

## Do not include sensitive information

Do not post:

- passwords;
- cookies;
- authentication tokens;
- private account information;
- payment information;
- private video or subtitle content you do not have permission to share.

## Privacy issues

For questions about data access, storage, microphone/audio use, or third-party requests, see the [SureLive Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

## Public issue tracker

Bug reports and compatibility problems can be filed in the **Issues** section of this repository.

Feature requests may also be filed there, but planned features are not guaranteed to be implemented.
